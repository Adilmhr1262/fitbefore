      How it works, and its limits

      • All dimensions are stored in centimetres and converted for display. 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly.
      • Clearance is the space dimension minus the furniture dimension. A clearance under 5 cm (about 2 in) is flagged as tight, so you can allow room for hands, skirting, or uneven walls.
      • Rotating swaps the furniture width and depth, so you can check the other orientation without retyping dimensions.
      • Dimensions only. A fit here does not mean the item will pass through doors, hallways, or stairwells, or that it can be assembled inside the space. Check the delivery route separately.
      • Privacy: no account, no backend, no analytics. The app makes no network requests of its own, so your measurements never leave this device unless you export a backup file.
